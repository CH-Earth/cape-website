Last Thursday, several lab members visited WA Ranches, located approximately 25 minutes Northwest of Calgary. [WA Ranches](https://vet.ucalgary.ca/wa-ranches) is a cattle ranch that was gifted to the University of Calgary in 2018 and is now being used as a teaching and research facility by several faculties including Veterinary Medicine, Science, and Engineering. 
Terri Whitehead led a tour of the ranch and showed some of Al’s and [Erin Nicholls’](https://profiles.ucalgary.ca/erin-nicholls) hydrologic monitoring equipment, including a rain gauge, a soil moisture and temperature probe, and an eddy covariance tower.
